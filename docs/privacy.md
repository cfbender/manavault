# ManaVault Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

This policy covers the ManaVault Android app (`dev.cfb.manavault`) published on
Google Play and GitHub releases, and the open-source ManaVault server software.
ManaVault is developed by Cody Bender ("we").

## Summary

ManaVault is self-hosted. The app is a client for a ManaVault server that you
(or someone you trust) run. Apart from a public demo server (see below), we do
not operate a service for app users, and we do not collect, receive, sell, or
share your personal data. The app contains no
advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## Data the app handles

- **Server URL.** The ManaVault server address you enter is stored on your
  device so the app can reconnect. You can change or clear it in the app.
- **Collection, deck, and account data.** Everything you view or edit in the
  app, including your sign-in, is sent to and stored on the ManaVault server
  you configured. That server's operator controls this data; we have no access
  to it.
- **Camera.** The card scanner uses the camera only while you have it open.
  Card recognition runs on your device. If you turn on the scanner's optional
  **Collect training data** setting (off by default), scanned frames and their
  labels are uploaded to your own ManaVault server.
- **Shared and opened files.** When you share or open a text or CSV file with
  ManaVault, the app reads it and sends its contents to your server to import.

## Public demo server

We run a demo server at `https://manavault-demo.cfb.dev` so app reviewers and
testers can try ManaVault without hosting their own. Everyone using the demo
shares one account, so anything entered there is visible to other demo users.
The demo is reset to sample data every night, which erases all changes. Do not
enter personal information on the demo server.

## Third-party services

To show card images, prices, and recommendations, the app loads content
directly from third-party services such as Scryfall, EDHREC, MTGStocks, and
GitHub (to check for app updates). These requests include standard network
information such as your IP address and device user agent, and are governed by
those services' own privacy policies. Links to stores and websites open in your
browser.

A ManaVault server may also contact third-party services that its operator
configures, such as card data providers, AI providers, or cloud backup
storage. Those connections are set up and controlled by the server operator.

## Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data. To delete data, clear the server URL in the
app or uninstall it, and delete your data on your ManaVault server (or ask its
operator to).

## Children

ManaVault is not directed at children under 13.
